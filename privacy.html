Privacy Policy

VoyageShelf · Last updated September 28, 2026

The short version: VoyageShelf does not collect or transmit any personal data. Everything you add to your shelf stays only on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. VoyageShelf has no servers, no accounts, and no sign-up. There is no data collection of any kind — not your name, not your email, not your location, not usage analytics.

Where your data lives

The places, dates, and notes you add are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to VoyageShelf, its developer, or any third party.

Location data

VoyageShelf does not access, request, or collect your device's location at any time.

Tracking and analytics

VoyageShelf contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

Sharing your shelf

When you choose to share an image of your bookshelf, that image is created entirely on your device and handed to Apple's standard share sheet. What happens next depends on where you choose to send it — for example, if you share to a social media app, that app's own privacy policy governs how it handles the image. VoyageShelf itself has no part in that transfer and keeps no record of it.

If you choose to save that image to your Photos library, VoyageShelf only adds the new image — it does not read, browse, or access any other photo already in your library.

Children's privacy

Because VoyageShelf does not collect any data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to voyageshelf@gmail.com.
